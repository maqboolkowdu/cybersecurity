Five modules, start to finish.

Each module links back here, so you can always find your way home. Open a module below, or come back any time to jump to a different one.

01

The World of Cybersecurity

Every day you generate data — photos, messages, medical visits, purchases — and every day someone, somewhere, wants a piece of it. This module lays the foundation for the whole course: what cybersecurity actually means, why your data is valuable, who is trying to take it, and why governments now treat digital defense as a matter of national security.

1.1 The World of Cybersecurity1.2 Organizational Data1.3 What Was Taken?1.4 Cyber Attackers1.5 Cyberwarfare

02

Attacks, Concepts and Techniques

Now that you understand why data is valuable, it's time to look at the toolbox attackers actually use. This module breaks down malware families, the social and technical tricks used to break in, how software and hardware vulnerabilities get exploited, and why cryptocurrency has become part of the modern threat landscape.

2.1 Analyzing a Cyber Attack2.2 Methods of Infiltration2.3 Security Vulnerability and Exploits2.4 The Cybersecurity Landscape

03

Protecting Your Data and Privacy

You now know what's at stake and how attackers operate. This module turns that knowledge into habits — the settings, tools, and everyday decisions that actually keep your devices, passwords, and personal data safe, plus what you're really agreeing to when you tap 'I Accept.'

3.1 Protecting Your Devices and Network3.2 Data Maintenance3.3 Who Owns Your Data?3.4 Safeguarding Your Online Privacy3.5 Discover Your Own Risky Online Behavior

04

Protecting the Organization

Individual habits matter, but organizations face attacks at a completely different scale. This module looks at the devices and technologies that guard a network perimeter, the behavior-based techniques that catch what signatures miss, and how a mature security team — using Cisco's own approach as a model — plans for, detects, and recovers from incidents.

4.1 Cybersecurity Devices and Technologies4.2 Behavior Approach to Cybersecurity4.3 Cisco's Approach to Cybersecurity

Will Your Future Be in Cybersecurity?

You've learned to think like a defender — and, a little, like an attacker. This final module asks what that skill set is actually worth: where the legal and ethical lines sit, and which certifications and career paths turn everything you've learned into a job.
